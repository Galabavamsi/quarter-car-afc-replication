What AFC does near zero error

Inside the envelope the logarithm is close to linear, so AFC acts like a cascade of proportional loops. As |zs| approaches μ1 the barrier raises the gain sharply. That is how the bound is enforced, and it is also why a slow or saturated valve turns AFC into bang-bang control.

Plant: Alleyne & Hedrick (1995) hydraulic quarter car with the servo-valve flow law of Na et al. eqs. (2)–(6). The valve gain Kv = 5×10−4 m/V and lag τ = 1/30 s are calibrated so that the unchanged Case A AFC reproduces the paper's Case 10 indices. The pressure scaling κ = 10−6 is assumed. The anti-windup and skyhook controllers are comparisons added by this project, not part of the paper.
Paper hardware numbers are measurements on the authors' rig. Simulated numbers come from this model. Source code: quarter-car-afc-replication.
